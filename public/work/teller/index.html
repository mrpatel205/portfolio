Approach

Process, sized to constraints

The programme was planned as one big-bang release covering every withdrawal, payment and deposit transaction type, but we designed them one at a time. Each ran its own design process, sized to its breadth and complexity, and to the time and capacity we actually had.

Some got deep discovery: time in branch, moderated testing and workshops with our SMEs. Others didn't have that luxury, so the process flexed to match.
