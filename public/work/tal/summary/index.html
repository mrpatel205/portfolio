Six months to turn an acquired income protection product into a Suncorp-branded MVP, starting with its 19-step quote.

Impact

The MVP shipped on deadline and on brand: six months, with five phases delivered in sprints.

6

5

Situation

TAL had acquired Suncorp’s life and income protection business and had six months to launch an MVP. The quote took 19 steps, and each one was a chance to drop out. The call-centre flow had to stay intact.

Task

I rebuilt the quote funnel on Suncorp’s design system, split into phases the agile team could ship, without breaking the backend or the call-centre handoff.
