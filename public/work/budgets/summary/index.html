Helping customers budget

Company
Major bank
Industry
Fin-Tech
Duration
Feb 24 to Jun 24
Channel
React mini web app

Budgets shipped and is live in the bank's app. I was its sole designer and researcher, from MVP to handover.

Read case study

Impact

Live in the bank's app. Around 4,000 customers logged in during August 2025, averaging 1.7 sessions a month, so adoption is modest so far.

Situation

Budgeting was a FY24 priority for the bank's customers, yet there was no way to track spending against a budget in-app. People used spreadsheets and self-made bucket accounts instead.

Task

I owned research, design, concept testing and interaction design end to end, working with the product owner, business analyst and engineering.
