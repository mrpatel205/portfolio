Situation

Twelve years of releases had added pages without stepping back. 12.7% of all clicks went on navigation alone, and with a move to product-led growth, trial users had to find value without a salesperson.

Task

I pitched the UX health of Buildxact to leadership as a structure problem, then led the UX team to create an experience vision in harmony with the product vision.
