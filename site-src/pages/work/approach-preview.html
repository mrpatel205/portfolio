The problem was already framed when I joined; how to solve it wasn't.

I ran two rounds of research and testing to define and refine the approach to the budgets feature and worked close with my BA and the accessibility team to produce the final detailed figma artefacts to hand over to engineering.

I'd never worked in insurance, so I started with immersion: whiteboarding the product landscape with my design lead, a gap analysis of TAL and Suncorp, and pulling apart both quote funnels.

From there I split the journey into five phases, Eligibility, Quick Quote, Underwriting, Review Application, Confirm & Pay, so the team could ship in sprints. Weekly showcases kept stakeholders new to agile in step.

I started with evidence rather than opinions: a top-task workshop built on page visits and feature clicks, and an audit of pages, navigation and calls to action.

Jesse James Garrett's five planes took us from strategy to structure to skeleton, and four principles tested every idea: Simplified, Personalised, Supported and Connected.
