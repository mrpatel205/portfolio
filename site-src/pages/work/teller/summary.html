Situation

A legacy system ran teller transactions in all 400+ of the bank's branches for nearly 40 years. It meant switching systems and memorising 4-digit codes, and it was too costly to maintain.

Task

I took on the Withdrawals epic halfway through the programme and owned Deposits from scratch, taking both to the new design system and a responsive engineering handover.
