Budgets is live in a major bank’s app. I was its sole designer and researcher, from early research through to engineering handover.

Read case study

Impact

Budgets is live in the major bank’s app. In August 2025, around 4,000 customers logged in, using it almost twice a month on average. These figures show early use, but don’t tell us why customers returned or whether Budgets changed their budgeting behaviour.

Situation

The major bank’s FY24 research identified budgeting as a priority. Customers tracked their money in spreadsheets and “bucket” accounts, but had no way to compare category spending with a budget in the app.

Task

I led the research and design, from exploring customer needs and testing concepts through to designing the interactions and preparing the handover with the product and engineering teams.
